Cross-Channel Campaign: Q4 Visibility Push

A multi-channel advertising campaign that increased brand recall by 38%.

The Challenge

A local brand needed to cut through the noise in a crowded Q4 market. Their existing advertising was fragmented across channels with no unified message or measurable strategy.

Our Approach

We designed a unified campaign architecture spanning digital, print, and experiential channels. Each channel had a consistent visual language but adapted to its specific audience behavior and context.

  • ✓Audience segmentation using behavioral and cultural data
  • ✓Unified creative direction across digital, print, and OOH
  • ✓Real-time optimization using performance data

The Results

The campaign achieved 38% higher brand recall than previous efforts and a 22% increase in qualified leads across all channels.
