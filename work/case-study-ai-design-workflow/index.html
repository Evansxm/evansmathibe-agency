AI-Powered Design Workflow

How we reduced design iteration time by 40% through automated mockup generation.

The Challenge

A creative agency was spending an average of 3 weeks on design iterations per campaign. Manual mockup generation, variant testing, and client feedback loops were consuming 60% of the team's productive capacity. The bottleneck wasn't talent — it was the repetitive computational work.

Our Approach

We built a custom AI workflow that handles the initial mockup generation, variant creation, and first-round client feedback processing. The system uses image generation models fine-tuned on the client's brand guidelines to produce on-brand outputs instantly.

  • ✓Fine-tuned image generation on brand color palettes and typography
  • ✓Automated variant creation for different channels and formats
  • ✓AI-powered client feedback parsing and iteration tracking

The Results

The client went from 3-week design cycles to 10-day cycles. The creative team regained capacity to focus on strategic direction and original concept work.
