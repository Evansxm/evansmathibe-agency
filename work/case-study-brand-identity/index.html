DesignCase Study

Brand Identity System

Complete visual identity rework for a Johannesburg brand.

The Challenge

A growing Johannesburg brand needed a unified visual identity that could scale across digital products, print materials, and environmental graphics. Their existing materials were inconsistent and lacked a cohesive system.

Our Approach

We started with cultural research into South African visual traditions, then built a design system around the brand's core values. The system included logo variations, color palettes, typography scales, and component libraries.

  • ✓Logo design with geometric language inspired by interconnected forms
  • ✓Color system built around the coral/salmon primary palette
  • ✓Scalable design components for digital and print

The Results

The brand achieved 95% visual consistency across all touchpoints and reduced design production time by 30%. The system has been adopted across all marketing channels.
