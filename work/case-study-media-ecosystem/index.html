Media Ecosystem: Content Strategy at Scale

Built a content ecosystem reaching 2M+ monthly impressions with culturally grounded storytelling.

The Challenge

A media brand needed to scale its content output without losing the cultural authenticity that made it resonate with its audience. The existing approach was fragmented and lacked a coherent editorial strategy.

Our Approach

We built an editorial framework rooted in South African cultural context, with AI-assisted content scheduling and distribution. The system balanced timely, culturally relevant content with evergreen brand pillars.

  • ✓Editorial calendar with AI-assisted topic ideation
  • ✓Cultural fluency audit of all content pillars
  • ✓Automated distribution across platforms and channels

The Results

The brand achieved 2M+ monthly impressions with a 45% increase in audience engagement and a 60% reduction in content production time.
