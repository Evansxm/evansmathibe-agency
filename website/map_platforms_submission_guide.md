### Recommended Address

Use **Sandton, Johannesburg, 2196, Gauteng** as the primary address. As a creative agency, Sandton is the business hub of South Africa and the most credible location. Use service-area setup on Google to avoid requiring a specific storefront address.

### Quick Tip: Consistency

Use the **exact same NAP (Name, Address, Phone)** across ALL platforms. Consistency is the #1 factor for local SEO citation quality. Variations like "EvansMathibe Agency" vs "Evans Mathibe Agency" will hurt your search rankings.
