## 1. Google Maps
**Submit / claim here:** https://www.google.com/business/
1. Sign in with a Google Account.
2. Go to https://www.google.com/business/
3. Click "Add your business" → "Enter your address manually".
4. Use the data from `submissions_google_maps.csv` for each location.
5. Add the Website URL: https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html
6. Verify via postcard, phone, or email.

## 2. Apple Maps
**Submit here:** https://mapsconnect.apple.com/
1. Sign in with an Apple ID at https://mapsconnect.apple.com/
2. Click "Add a new place".
3. Enter each location from `submissions_apple_maps.csv`.
4. Set Website to https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html
5. Submit for review.

## 3. Bing Places
**Submit here:** https://www.bingplaces.com/
1. Sign in at https://www.bingplaces.com/
2. Click "Add a new listing".
3. Fill in data from `submissions_bing_places.csv`.
4. Attach the website: https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html
5. Verify via phone or postcard.

## 4. Foursquare
**Submit here:** https://foursquare.com/add-place
1. Go to https://foursquare.com/add-place
2. Search for the venue first — if not found, click "Add it".
3. Use data from `submissions_foursquare.csv`.
4. Add Website: https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html
5. Foursquare will review and approve.

## 5. Yelp
**Submit here:** https://biz.yelp.com/
1. Go to https://biz.yelp.com/ and click "Add your business to Yelp".
2. Search for the location; if not found, add it manually.
3. Use data from `submissions_yelp.csv`.
4. Website: https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html
5. Verify via phone call or postcard.

## 6. HERE Maps
**Submit here:** https://maps.here.com/ — then click "Add a missing place"
1. Go to https://maps.here.com/
2. Search for the area, click "Add a missing place".
3. Use data from `submissions_here_tomtom.csv` (HERE rows).
4. Website: https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html

## 7. TomTom
**Submit here:** https://mapshare.tomtom.com/
1. Go to https://mapshare.tomtom.com/
2. Click "Add a new road or place".
3. Enter data from `submissions_here_tomtom.csv` (TomTom rows).
4. Website: https://evansxm.github.io/em/index.html

## 8. SA Business Directories
**Common directories:**
- **Yellow Pages SA:** https://www.yellowpages.co.za/ — Register and "Add Business"
- **SA Business Guide:** https://www.sabusinessguide.co.za/list-business
- **My Business Directory:** https://www.mybusinessdirectory.co.za/
- **Hotfrog SA:** https://www.hotfrog.co.za/
- **Cylex SA:** https://www.cylex.co.za/

Use `submissions_sa_directories.csv` for all directory submissions.
