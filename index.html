About the Organisation

At the National Youth Centre (NYC), we don't just see youth as the leaders of tomorrow—we see you as the heartbeat of today. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful conviction: every single young person in South Africa, from the bustling streets of Jo'burg to the quietest corners of our rural provinces, deserves a real shot at greatness.

Our mission is deeply rooted in the National Youth Policy (NYP 2020-2030). We act as a vital bridge, translating national vision into local action. By working hand-in-hand with provincial and municipal structures, we ensure that the policy's pillars—economic transformation, education, health, and social cohesion—aren't just words on a page, but lived realities for our youth.
