# Advertising Industry Integration Strategy: Evans Mathibe Agency

## Target Industry Directories & Platforms
To ensure visibility in B2B procurement and advertising searches, the agency will be listed on the following platforms:

### 1. Creative & Advertising Directories
- **IDIDTHAT.co:** Primary directory for SA creative agencies.
- **MarkLives #Directory:** Essential for industry mapping and corporate visibility.
- **Black Agencies Directory:** Critical for B-BBEE sensitive procurement.
- **Sortlist South Africa:** For matching with corporate project needs.

### 2. Professional Associations (Vetting & Credibility)
- **Association for Communication and Advertising (ACA):** Membership targets for 2026.
- **IAB South Africa:** Focus on Digital & AI Automation credibility.
- **Marketing Association of South Africa (MASA):** Alignment with professional marketing standards.

### 3. Government & Precinct Mapping
- **Pretoria Government Precinct:** Focus on proximity to departmental procurement offices.
- **Cape Town CBD:** Alignment with tech and creative innovation hubs.

## B2B Procurement Optimization
- **B-BBEE Status:** Documented as a black-owned agency (Level 1 contributor).
- **Service Specialization:** Highlight AI Brand Automation as a unique differentiator in procurement bids.
- **Case Studies:** Focus on high-transaction districts like Sandton and Rosebank to demonstrate local impact.
