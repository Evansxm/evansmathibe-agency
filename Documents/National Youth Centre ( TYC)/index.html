About the National Youth Centre

The National Youth Centre (NYC) is at the forefront of youth empowerment in South Africa. We are committed to equipping the next generation with the skills and opportunities they need to thrive in a rapidly evolving world, focusing on the critical pillars of the National Youth Policy (NYP 2020-2030).

Our programs are designed to bridge the gap between potential and achievement, fostering innovation, digital literacy, and entrepreneurial spirit. We work closely with national, provincial, and municipal stakeholders to ensure our initiatives have a lasting, positive impact across the country.
